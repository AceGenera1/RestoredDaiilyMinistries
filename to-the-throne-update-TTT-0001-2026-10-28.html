TO THE THRONE · TTT-0001 · WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 28, 2026

Intercession Update Letter

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ, when we shared Monday’s Weekly Intercession Letter, we invited you to remember people living through violence, displacement, shortages, and illness. As the week continues, some situations have developed, while others remain painfully unresolved. I want to share what we have learned since Monday and encourage you to keep these families and communities before our Heavenly Father. We do not have to wait for another headline to continue standing in the gap.

01 Families Affected by Strikes in Nyala

Nyala, Sudan

New development reported October 28th

Following strikes in Nyala on October 26th, Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders) reported receiving 36 bodies and treating more than 150 wounded people. MSF attributed the strikes to the Sudanese Armed Forces. Other reports suggested a higher death toll, but those additional figures had not been independently verified. Families are grieving, survivors need medical care, and local medical teams are responding under enormous pressure.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for the families grieving these losses and for those recovering from injuries. Remember the medical teams caring for the wounded and ask the Lord for safe humanitarian access, protection of civilians, and truthful investigation and accountability.

Isaiah 41:10 (WEB) — Don’t you be afraid, for I am with you. Don’t be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you. Yes, I will help you. Yes, I will uphold you with the right hand of my righteousness.

02 Continued Care During Reported Ebola Outbreaks

Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, and Nigeria

Continuing need — reports from different dates

The Ebola-related reports we shared on Monday still call for careful attention and prayer. The World Health Organization’s October 23rd bulletin presented Democratic Republic of the Congo figures dated October 20th, while later national reporting used different dates and cannot simply be added to those totals. Reports also described dangers facing people involved in outbreak response. These are the historical reports considered at the time, not a present-day assessment.

Prayer Focus: Please remember patients and families grieving loved ones, along with the healthcare workers and contact-tracing teams serving their communities. Let’s ask the Lord for effective treatment, protection from infection, clear public-health communication, and wisdom for those leading the response.

Psalm 107:20 (WEB) — He sends his word, and heals them, and delivers them from their graves.

03 Families Still Facing Humanitarian Shortages

Gaza Strip

Continuing humanitarian need

OCHA’s October 26th reporting continued to describe shortages of food assistance and medical supplies, together with risks to reliable water access. Some bakeries had reopened, which was a limited encouraging development, but it did not mean the wider humanitarian needs were resolved. Children and displaced families continued to rely on safe access to everyday necessities.

Prayer Focus: Let’s lift up families seeking food, clean water, shelter, and medical services. Pray that humanitarian assistance reaches people safely and consistently, and remember the children, displaced households, and aid workers still carrying these burdens.

Isaiah 58:7 (WEB) — Isn’t it to distribute your bread to the hungry, and that you bring the poor who are cast out to your house? When you see the naked, that you cover him; and that you not hide yourself from your own flesh?

04 Water and Healthcare Needs Remain in Taiz

Taiz, Yemen

Continuing need — no separate major change verified

The situation in Taiz remained a matter for prayer. UNICEF had warned that fighting and fuel shortages threatened water systems serving millions of people, while displaced families and impaired health services faced ongoing strain. As of this Wednesday review, a separate, verified material change since Monday had not been established. That absence of a new headline does not mean the needs had gone away.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for safe drinking water, fuel, medical supplies, and dependable care for children and displaced families. Remember clinics and nutrition programs, and ask the Lord to strengthen those serving vulnerable communities and protect civilians from further violence.

Isaiah 41:17 (WEB) — The poor and needy seek water, and there is none. Their tongue fails for thirst. I, the LORD, will answer them. I, the God of Israel, will not forsake them.

05 Families Awaiting Answers After the Abyei Attacks

Amiet Market and Todach, Abyei Region

Continuing need — casualty verification unresolved

Following the October 19th drone attacks near Amiet Market and Todach, UNISFA’s reporting established that civilians had been killed or wounded, while casualty figures and responsibility remained disputed. As families continued to mourn and wait for information about missing loved ones, the need for protection and an honest investigation remained urgent.

Prayer Focus: Please continue to remember families who are grieving or waiting for news of someone missing. Pray for the wounded and emergency responders, for protection in market communities, and for truth, justice, restraint, and lasting peace.

Isaiah 58:6 (WEB) — Isn’t this the fast that I have chosen: to release the bonds of wickedness, to undo the straps of the yoke, to let the oppressed go free, and that you break every yoke?

These updates are reminders that the work of intercession does not end when a report is published. There are still families grieving, people awaiting answers, and communities hoping for safety and relief. Please carry these matters before our Heavenly Father, and allow the Holy Spirit to guide you beyond the prayer focuses shared here.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
