TO THE THRONE · TTT-0003 · November 9th, 2026

Episode Recording Notes

Five matters for intercession · Prepared for smooth spoken delivery

Key Verified Details   |   Prayer Focus   |   Full Scripture
01

Civilian Families After Airstrikes

Sanaa area, Yemen

Key Verified Details

According to a November 9th Associated Press report, Houthi health authorities reported that 44 people were killed and 40 were wounded following strikes on homes east of Sanaa, Yemen. These figures were attributed to the health authorities and had not been independently verified. Children were among those reported killed, and affected families and rescue workers continued facing urgent needs.

Prayer Focus

Let’s pray for grieving families and people recovering from injuries. Remember the children affected by the strikes, and ask the Lord to protect civilians, medical teams, and rescue workers.

Scripture · Psalm 147:3 (WEB)

He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.
02

Displaced Families in Urgent Need of Shelter

Sudan

Key Verified Details

On November 6th, UNHCR and the International Organization for Migration warned that funding shortages threatened emergency shelter assistance across Sudan. The agencies estimated that 15.4 million people needed shelter or essential household supplies, while available funds allowed aid partners to prioritize only about ten percent of those needs.

Prayer Focus

Let’s remember families without dependable shelter. Pray for bedding, household supplies, strength for local aid partners, and safe humanitarian access for people who urgently need help.

Scripture · Psalm 46:1 (WEB)

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
03

Flooding in Displacement Shelters

Gaza Strip

Key Verified Details

In its November 8th report, OCHA described heavy rains that began on November 6th and flooded displacement sites across Gaza. Thousands of temporary shelters were damaged, while essential services were disrupted. Humanitarian organizations also reported that supplies such as tents, blankets, tarpaulins, and clothing were nearly exhausted.

Prayer Focus

Let’s pray for families whose temporary shelters have been flooded or damaged. Remember children, older adults, and people with disabilities, and ask the Lord for dry shelter, sanitation, medical care, and safe delivery of relief.

Scripture · Psalm 121:5 (WEB)

The LORD is your keeper. The LORD is your shade on your right hand.
04

Households Recovering From Flooding

Brevard County, Florida, United States

Key Verified Details

After heavy rainfall on November 2nd, residents in parts of Brevard County, Florida, were dealing with flooding and damaged homes. County notices issued on November 4th and 5th described shelter resources and cleanup assistance. This was continuing recovery work, not a new disaster on November 9th.

Prayer Focus

Let’s lift up residents working to clean up damaged homes and restore safe living conditions. Pray especially for older adults and people with disabilities who need extra help, and for responders and volunteers assisting them.

Scripture · Psalm 34:6 (WEB)

This poor man cried, and the LORD heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles.
05

Children Whose School Days Are Interrupted by Air-Raid Alarms

Ukraine

Key Verified Details

According to a November 3rd UNICEF report, air-raid warnings affected nearly two-thirds of school days in Ukraine during September and October. Repeated alerts disrupted education and placed additional strain on students, teachers, and families trying to maintain safe learning environments.

Prayer Focus

Let’s pray for students, parents, and teachers facing repeated interruptions. Ask the Lord for protection, safe schools and shelters, and care for children and families dealing with the emotional effects of continued alarms.

Scripture · Psalm 82:3 (WEB)

Defend the weak, the poor, and the fatherless. Maintain the rights of the poor and oppressed.
