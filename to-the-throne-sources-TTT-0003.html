TO THE THRONE · TTT-0003 · NOVEMBER 9, 2026

Public Sources & Verification

Original reporting, publication dates, and attribution for the five matters in the November 9 Weekly Intercession Letter. Multiple reports sharing the same underlying source are identified as such.

01 — Yemen — Civilian families near Sanaa

Reported factual basis

Associated Press reported November 9 that Houthi health authorities raised their reported toll from Saturday’s strikes on homes east of Sanaa to 44 people killed, including children, and 40 wounded. These figures are attributed to the Houthi authorities, not independently verified findings; families and rescue teams continue to face urgent needs.

Sources and what they establish

Associated Press · November 9, 2026

Reports the revised health-authorities toll, attributed rather than independently verified.

Earlier attributed toll, superseded by the November 9 update; these figures must not be added together.

https://apnews.com/article/1b5aef5b86be2e96e1cd8a18165afaf6

02 — Sudan — Families without emergency shelter

Reported factual basis

UNHCR and IOM warned on November 6 that shortages in emergency-shelter funding threaten displaced families and returning households across Sudan. The agencies estimate that 15.4 million people need shelter or essential household supplies, while available funds support prioritizing only about 10 percent of those needs.

Sources and what they establish

UNHCR and IOM · November 6, 2026

Joint firsthand announcement of estimated shelter needs and funding limitations.

https://www.unhcr.org/news/press-releases/unhcr-and-iom-warn-millions-sudan-risk-losing-emergency-shelter-funding-runs
Anadolu Agency · November 6, 2026

Reporting dependent on the same UN agencies’ estimate; not independent corroboration.

https://mobile.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/154m-people-in-sudan-urgently-need-shelter-as-funding-shortages-threaten-aid-un-agencies/4114415

03 — Gaza — Families in flooded temporary shelters

Reported factual basis

OCHA’s November 8 humanitarian report describes heavy rains beginning November 6 that flooded displacement sites, damaged thousands of temporary shelters, and disrupted essential services in Gaza. Humanitarian partners report that tents, tarpaulins, blankets, and clothing are nearly exhausted, leaving displaced families at heightened risk.

Sources and what they establish

UN OCHA · Report dated November 8; posted November 9, 2026

Primary situation report on flooding, shelter damage, and shortages of humanitarian supplies.

https://cdnassets.aa.gss.superonline.net/en/world/heavy-rains-flood-gaza-shelters-putting-displaced-families-at-immediate-risk-ocha/4115188

04 — United States — Brevard County, Florida flood recovery

Reported factual basis

Heavy rainfall on November 2 caused flooding across parts of Brevard County, Florida, leaving residents with housing and cleanup needs. County notices from November 4 and 5 describe shelter resources and flood cleanup assistance, confirming an ongoing recovery effort rather than a new November 9 disaster.

05 — Ukraine — Children’s education disrupted by air-raid alarms

Reported factual basis

UNICEF reported November 3 that air-raid alarms affected nearly two-thirds of school days during September and October, interrupting education across Ukraine. Repeated alerts and insecurity continue to strain children, teachers, and families seeking safe, consistent learning.

Wednesday, November 11th — Intercession Update Verification

This update documents two material developments within the five prayer matters first covered in the November 9th letter. Gaza's November 11th appeal was issued after Monday's letter; Brevard County recovery services were announced on November 9th and are distinguished from the earlier flood itself. Yemen, Sudan and Ukraine remained continuing prayer matters, not new Wednesday developments. The sources below preserve publication dates and the scope of each claim.

Gaza Strip — November 11th emergency winter-supply appeal

UN OCHA, Humanitarian Situation Report: Heavy Rains in the Gaza Strip · Published November 9, 2026; reporting on conditions through November 8th.

Primary UN humanitarian reporting establishes flooding beginning November 6th, damage to displacement sites, severe shortages of shelter supplies, and appeals from over 26,000 households. This last figure counts appeals for assistance, not fully assessed households.

Read the OCHA situation report
UN OCHA, Statement by Emergency Relief Coordinator Tom Fletcher · Published November 11, 2026.

Primary UN statement identifying the renewed urgent request for durable shelter, warm clothing, blankets, heating supplies, fuel, pumping equipment and other winter response materials. The statement is an appeal, not confirmation that these supplies arrived.

Read the November 11th statement

Brevard County, Florida — Recovery assistance announced November 9th

Brevard County official Storm Center — Multi-Agency Resource Centers · Published November 9, 2026.

Local government primary evidence that flood assistance centers opened November 9th in Palm Bay and West Melbourne to connect survivors with available recovery services and partner agencies. It does not establish that all recovery needs have been met.

Read county storm and recovery notices
Brevard County Storm Center — Cleanup Kits and Debris Collection · Announcements dated November 9, 2026.

Same local-government reporting channel confirms cleanup-kit distribution at selected county libraries and flood-related debris-removal arrangements with curbside collections scheduled to begin November 10th in specified areas. These items share a source and are not independent corroboration.

Read county cleanup and debris notices

Continuing matters

Yemen, Sudan and Ukraine: no separately verified material new development was established in the approved Wednesday review. The sources for their original Monday reporting remain above in this weekly verification record. The continued request for prayer is not a claim that any situation has been resolved or has newly worsened.
