TO THE THRONE · TTT-0001 · October 26, 2026

Weekly Intercession Letter

Standing in the gap for those in need. Turning awareness into intercession.

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

As I write to you this week, I am reminded that while the needs of this world may be separated by great distances, the people facing them are never beyond the reach of our prayers. Families are grieving, communities are searching for safety, and healthcare workers are caring for people in difficult circumstances. I want to share five matters with you, not simply as reports to read, but as people and situations we can faithfully bring before our Heavenly Father.

01 Families Affected by Drone Attacks

Amiet Market, Abyei Region, Sudan–South Sudan border

UNISFA reported that drone attacks near Amiet Market and Todach on October 19 killed and injured civilians, including women and children. Some people were still missing, and the full casualty figures remained under verification. Behind those reports are families waiting for answers and communities carrying the weight of loss.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for the families who are grieving and for everyone who has been wounded. Remember those who are still missing, and ask the Lord to protect civilians, bring truthful answers, and lead these communities toward peace.

Psalm 34:18 (WEB) — The Lord is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.

02 Families Grieving After Reported Civilian Killings

Tagadayat, Ménaka Region, Mali

Mali’s armed forces reported finding 54 civilian bodies and evacuating nine survivors in Tagadayat. The reported attribution to Islamic State in the Greater Sahara had not been independently verified. Families and neighboring communities continue to face grief, uncertainty, and fear.

Prayer Focus: Please remember the bereaved families and survivors. Pray for protection for vulnerable communities, aid for displaced households, and an end to further violence.

Psalm 9:9 (WEB) — The Lord will also be a high tower for the oppressed; a high tower in times of trouble.

03 Families Facing Severe Humanitarian Needs

Gaza Strip

OCHA reported that approximately 950,000 displaced people faced medium-to-high food risk, while shelter supplies for the rainy season remained severely limited. Shortages also threatened health and water services. These conditions leave families, especially children, in urgent need of care and security.

Prayer Focus: Let’s lift up the children, parents, and other civilians enduring these conditions. Pray for food, clean water, shelter, medicine, safe humanitarian access, and the protection of those serving people in need. Ask the Lord to bring healing, restraint, and peace.

Psalm 145:14 (WEB) — The Lord upholds all who fall, and raises up all those who are bowed down.

04 Communities Struggling for Water and Medical Care

Taiz, Yemen

UNICEF reported that fighting and fuel shortages threatened water systems serving approximately three million people around Taiz. Displacement continued, and many health facilities were impaired. Families who need clean water and medical treatment are living with serious uncertainty.

Prayer Focus: Pray especially for children and families displaced by conflict. Let’s ask the Lord to provide water, fuel, medicine, and oxygen; strengthen hospitals and healthcare workers; and protect civilians while peace is pursued.

Psalm 46:1 (WEB) — God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

05 Patients and Communities Facing an Ebola Outbreak

Bundibugyo outbreak, DR Congo, Kenya, Nigeria, and the wider region

Reports presented in this week’s original letter described 9,704 confirmed cases and 4,687 deaths in the Democratic Republic of the Congo as of October 20, as well as reported cases in Kenya and Nigeria. The original account also noted a vaccine trial beginning October 23. These are the figures and claims recorded in the historical weekly report, not a new assessment of current outbreak conditions.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for patients and grieving families, for healthcare workers and response teams, and for the protection of communities. Ask the Lord to give public-health leaders wisdom and to guide research and treatment efforts.

Psalm 41:3 (WEB) — The Lord will sustain him on his sickbed, and restore him from his bed of illness.

As always, brothers and sisters, these are merely five of the many people and situations around the world in need of prayer. Let us stand in the gap for them and intercede on their behalf before the Throne. Take these matters before our Heavenly Father this week, and we encourage you to pray beyond these prayer prompts. Allow the Holy Spirit to guide your prayers and help you intercede for the people and situations represented here.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
