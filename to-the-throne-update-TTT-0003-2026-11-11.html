TO THE THRONE · TTT-0003 · WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 11, 2026

Intercession Update Letter

New developments in Gaza and Florida · Continuing prayer for Yemen, Sudan, and Ukraine

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

On Monday, we brought five situations before the Lord, remembering families whose lives have been affected by violence, displacement, severe weather, and uncertainty. As we reach the middle of the week, two of those matters have developments worth sharing: an urgent appeal for winter relief supplies in Gaza and organized flood-recovery assistance in Brevard County, Florida. The needs we raised in Yemen, Sudan, and Ukraine have not faded, even where no new development has been established. Let us continue to pray with understanding and compassion for every person involved.

01 Urgent Appeal for Winter Supplies After Flooding

Gaza Strip

New development · November 11th UN appeal

Heavy rains beginning on November 6th flooded displacement sites across Gaza, damaged tents and makeshift shelters, and disrupted essential services. In a report published November 9th, the United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs said humanitarian organizations had received requests for help from more than 26,000 households reporting damage to their shelters or belongings by November 8th. That figure represents requests for assistance, rather than a completed assessment of all affected families. The UN also reported that available stocks of basic shelter supplies inside Gaza were almost exhausted while many families were exposed to worsening weather.

On November 11th, UN humanitarian chief Tom Fletcher renewed an urgent appeal for the entry of essential winter supplies. His statement called for durable shelters, warm clothing, blankets, bedding, safe heating equipment, fuel, and machinery needed to remove floodwater and debris. This appeal is a significant new development in the ongoing emergency: families who have already endured displacement now face further exposure to rain and cold, while relief organizations warn that needed supplies and equipment are not reaching people quickly enough.

Prayer Focus: Let's remember families who have lost or damaged their temporary shelters, especially children, older adults, and people with disabilities. Pray for warm, dry places to stay, reliable food and medical care, safe sanitation, and the swift arrival of appropriate humanitarian supplies. Ask the Lord to protect relief workers and families still exposed to flooding and unsafe conditions.

Psalm 9:9 (WEB) — The LORD will also be a high tower for the oppressed; a high tower in times of trouble.

02 Community Recovery Assistance After Flooding

Brevard County, Florida, United States

Recovery response · Services launched November 9th

Families across parts of Brevard County are continuing to recover from flooding that affected homes and neighborhoods earlier this month. On November 9th, county officials announced that two multi-agency resource centers had opened in Palm Bay and West Melbourne to connect flood survivors with recovery assistance. Representatives from organizations including the American Red Cross and state and local agencies were made available to help residents learn about services and next steps. These centers were part of the response to the earlier flooding, not evidence of a new flood event on November 11th.

The county also announced distribution of flood cleanup kits at selected public libraries beginning November 9th. The kits include basic supplies for cleaning and disinfecting damaged homes. County contractors began arranging debris removal from flood-affected areas, with curbside collection of eligible materials scheduled to start November 10th in specified unincorporated neighborhoods. These efforts represent practical progress in the response, but many residents still face the work of cleaning damaged homes, securing essential belongings, and returning to safe living conditions.

Prayer Focus: Please lift up households working through flood damage, especially those who need additional help because of age, disability, health, or limited resources. Pray for safe housing, sufficient cleanup assistance, protection from illness and injury during recovery, and strength for local responders, volunteers, and neighbors serving affected families.

Galatians 6:2 (WEB) — Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.

Continuing Intercession: Yemen, Sudan, and Ukraine

We must also continue remembering the three remaining matters from Monday's letter. Families in the Sanaa area of Yemen are grieving after reported airstrikes; displaced households in Sudan still face an immense shortage of adequate shelter and basic necessities; and children and teachers in Ukraine continue navigating education disrupted by repeated air-raid alarms. The reporting reviewed for this update did not establish a separate, material new development for these three situations. Their ongoing needs remain just as worthy of faithful intercession.

Prayer Focus: Let us continue lifting up grieving families and injured civilians in Yemen, displaced people and humanitarian workers in Sudan, and students, teachers, and parents in Ukraine. Pray for protection, practical provision, healing, opportunities for children to learn safely, and peace. The November 9th Weekly Intercession Letter contains the fuller circumstances and original Scripture for each matter.

A Wednesday update does not need five new headlines for our prayers to remain faithful. Where new information gives us a clearer picture, let us respond with specific intercession. Where suffering continues without a new announcement, let us not forget the people behind the stories. May we keep bringing these needs before our Heavenly Father throughout the remainder of this week.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
