Week of November 2, 2026 · TTT-0002

Weekly Intercession Letter

Five matters for prayer: Ukraine, West Bank, Djibouti/Yemen, Myanmar, and Sudan.

THE NEWS CYCLE MAY MOVE ON, BUT PRAYER NEVER CEASES.

View full-size graphicLetter PDFBack to weekly archive

Accessible Text · Weekly Intercession Letter

To the Body of Christ: this week we lift five needs before the Lord. Take these matters to prayer and allow the Holy Spirit to guide your intercession.

01 — Drone Strike on Railway Transportation

Dnipro, Ukraine

Ukrainian police reported an October 29 drone strike on railway transportation in Dnipro. Initial police reporting counted 13 deaths and 10 injuries; subsequent local accounts reported a higher death toll. Emergency personnel attended injured passengers and affected railway workers.

Take it before the throne: Comfort grieving families, including railway workers. • Heal injured passengers and strengthen emergency and medical teams. • Protect travelers, civilian transportation, and surrounding communities.

Psalm 82:3 (WEB) — Defend the weak, the poor, and the fatherless. Maintain the rights of the poor and oppressed.

02 — Displaced Children and School Access

West Bank, including East Jerusalem

UNICEF reports that displacement across the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, averaged 17 people, including eight children, each day in 2026. UNICEF also reports 84 schools under pending demolition or stop-work orders.

Take it before the throne: Protect children, families, teachers, and all civilians. • Provide safe shelter and stability for displaced families. • Keep education accessible and secure; guide humanitarian workers.

Proverbs 31:8 (WEB) — Open your mouth for the mute, in the cause of all who are left desolate.

03 — Dangerous Sea Crossings and Displacement

Djibouti / Yemen / Horn of Africa

The International Organization for Migration reported at least 139 deaths in an October 8–9 shipwreck off Djibouti. UNHCR separately reported more than 134,000 sea arrivals during January–September 2026 and described ongoing humanitarian risks along regional maritime routes.

Take it before the throne: Rescue and protect people making dangerous sea crossings. • Provide food, safe shelter, and compassionate assistance for displaced families. • Comfort loved ones awaiting news, and strengthen aid and rescue teams.

Psalm 107:28–30 (WEB) — 28 Then they cry to the LORD in their trouble, and he brings them out of their distress. 29 He makes the storm a calm, so that its waves are still. 30 Then they are glad because it is calm, so he brings them to their desired haven.

04 — Civilian Safety and Detention

Myanmar

In an October 30 Associated Press interview, United Nations human-rights investigator Kelley Currie described attacks on civilians, widespread detention, and fear among communities in Myanmar.

Take it before the throne: Protect children, displaced families, minority communities, and local churches. • Provide humane care, justice, and freedom for people unjustly detained. • Strengthen humanitarian workers and bring reconciliation and peace.

Isaiah 1:17 (WEB) — Learn to do well. Seek justice. Relieve the oppressed. Defend the fatherless. Plead for the widow.

05 — Children Killed at a Displacement Camp

West Kordofan, Sudan

UNICEF reported that at least nine children aged five to ten were killed in an October 17 attack on a camp housing displaced families in West Kordofan State. Families affected by violence and displacement continue to need safety and care.

Take it before the throne: Comfort grieving parents and families and provide trauma care. • Protect children and displaced families in camps and shelters. • Provide food, medical support, refuge, accountability, and peace.

Matthew 5:9 (WEB) — Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
