The Heart of the Ministry

Restored Daily Ministries exists to bring the light, hope, and truth of Christ Jesus to people facing every form of ailment, disability, hardship, and life limitation around the world.

Restored Daily Ministries exists to bring the light, hope, and truth of Christ Jesus to people facing every form of ailment, disability, hardship, and life limitation around the world.

Through Bible teaching, devotionals, prayer, and the preaching of the Gospel, our mission is to remind people that no condition, struggle, disability, or circumstance can disqualify them from being used by God.

We believe that Christ Jesus still calls, equips, strengthens, and works through ordinary people in extraordinary ways for His glory.

No matter the challenge, there is still purpose in Christ.
