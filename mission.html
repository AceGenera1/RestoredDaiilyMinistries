Mission

Restored in Christ. Renewed daily. Sent on mission.

The Heart of the Ministry

Restored Daily Ministries exists to bring the light, hope, and truth of Christ Jesus to people facing every form of ailment, disability, hardship, limitation, addiction, recovery, grief, and spiritual battle.

Through Bible teaching, devotionals, prayer, preaching, podcast encouragement, testimony, and discipleship, our heart is to remind people that no condition, struggle, disability, or circumstance can disqualify them from being used by God.

We are not the source of restoration. Christ Jesus is. This ministry simply exists as a vessel to point people back to Him.
