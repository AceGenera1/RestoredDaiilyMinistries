TO THE THRONE · TTT-0003 · November 9, 2026

Weekly Intercession Letter

Standing in the gap for those in need. Turning awareness into intercession.

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

As I write to you this week, I’m thinking about families whose lives have been interrupted by violence, displacement, destructive weather, and uncertainty. Some are grieving. Some are searching for a safe place to stay. Others are trying to help their children find some sense of normal life. I want to share five needs with you—not to overwhelm you with reports, but to invite us to remember the people behind them and faithfully take these situations before our Heavenly Father.

01 Civilian Families After Airstrikes

Sanaa area, Yemen

According to November 9 reporting by the Associated Press, Houthi health authorities reported 44 people killed and 40 wounded following strikes near Sanaa. The figures and responsibility had not been independently verified. Whatever the final accounting, families are facing injuries, loss, and fear.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for injured children and adults, for those mourning loved ones, and for families trying to find safety. Ask the Lord to strengthen medical workers and protect civilians and rescue teams.

Psalm 147:3 (WEB) — He heals the broken in heart, and binds up their wounds.

02 Displaced Families in Urgent Need of Shelter

Sudan

UNHCR and the International Organization for Migration estimated that 15.4 million people needed emergency shelter or essential household items. Limited funding meant aid partners could prioritize only about 10% of that need. These are agency estimates, but they describe a tremendous burden on displaced families.

Prayer Focus: Please remember families without dependable shelter. Pray for bedding, household supplies, safe places to stay, strength for local aid partners, and access to assistance for people who are too easily overlooked.

Psalm 46:1 (WEB) — God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

03 Flooding in Displacement Shelters

Gaza Strip

OCHA’s November 8 report described heavy rains flooding displacement sites and damaging thousands of temporary shelters. Humanitarian supplies were running low, leaving families already displaced in need of protection and practical care.

Prayer Focus: Let’s lift up families whose temporary shelters have been damaged, especially children, older adults, and others at risk. Pray for dry shelter, sanitation, food, medical supplies, and safe delivery of humanitarian assistance.

Psalm 121:5 (WEB) — The LORD is your keeper. The LORD is your shade on your right hand.

04 Households Recovering From Flooding

Brevard County, Florida, United States

Following flooding on November 2, Brevard County emergency notices described continuing shelter, cleanup, and recovery assistance for affected residents. This is an ongoing recovery need, not a new November 9 flood. Families are working to recover safe living conditions and rebuild their routines.

Prayer Focus: Please pray for households cleaning up damaged homes, for safe water and shelter, and for neighbors who need extra support, including older adults and people with disabilities. Remember the responders and volunteers assisting them.

Psalm 34:6 (WEB) — This poor man cried, and the LORD heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles.

05 Children Whose School Days Are Interrupted by Air-Raid Alarms

Ukraine

UNICEF reported that air-raid warnings affected nearly two-thirds of school days during September and October, disrupting children’s education. Students, parents, and teachers are trying to preserve learning and a measure of stability amid repeated interruptions.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for the protection of students and teachers, for safe schools and shelters, and for children and families carrying the emotional weight of continued alarms and uncertainty.

Psalm 82:3 (WEB) — Defend the weak, the poor, and the fatherless. Maintain the rights of the poor and oppressed.

These are only five of the many people and situations around the world in need of prayer. Let us stand in the gap for them and intercede on their behalf before the Throne. Take these matters before our Heavenly Father this week, and I encourage you to pray beyond these prayer focuses. Allow the Holy Spirit to guide your prayers as you intercede for the people and situations represented here.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
