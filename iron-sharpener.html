I.R.O.N. STUDY PROMPTS

I — Inspect the Scripture
Read carefully and understand the passage in context.

R — Reflect Prayerfully
Slow down, meditate on the Word, and listen humbly before the Lord.

O — Observe God’s Truth
What does this reveal about God, Christ, sin, faith, obedience, or grace?

N — Next Steps in Obedience
How is the Holy Spirit leading you to respond through faith, repentance, action, encouragement, or obedience?

Ask the Lord for wisdom, discernment, and a willing heart to obey His Word.

Prayerful Response

Ask the Lord:

• What are You teaching me through this passage?
• Is there something I need to repent of, surrender, believe, obey, or share?
• How should this change the way I live today?

“Lord Jesus, help me not only hear Your Word, but walk in obedience to it.”

Study Reminder

  • Read slowly and carefully.
  • Stay rooted in the context of Scripture.
  • Let Scripture interpret Scripture.
  • Pray for wisdom, discernment, and humility.
  • Listen for the guidance of the Holy Spirit through God’s Word.
