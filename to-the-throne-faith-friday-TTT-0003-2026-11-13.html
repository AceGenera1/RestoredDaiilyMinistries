TO THE THRONE · TTT-0003 · FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2026

Faith Friday: Prayer Never Ceases

As another week closes, let us keep standing in the gap.

To The Throne · Weekend Call to Prayer

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

As another week draws to a close, I want to encourage you not to leave the people we have remembered in prayer behind when the week ends. Their needs do not disappear when a report grows quiet, and our opportunity to bring them before the Lord does not end when Friday arrives.

Please continue to remember the families affected by the five urgent matters in this week’s Weekly Intercession Letter. Lift up those grieving the loss of loved ones, those trying to find safety and shelter, children whose lives have been disrupted, and communities struggling to recover. Ask the Lord to strengthen those who are weary and to guide the people bringing help where it is needed.

And please remember the Christian families in Nigeria’s Plateau State featured in this week’s Persecuted Church Prayer Watch. Pray for comfort for the bereaved, healing for the injured, safety for families and places of worship, wisdom and integrity for those responsible for protection, and repentance and salvation in Christ for those who commit violence. We do not need to resolve disputed accounts to pray for those who are hurting.

1 Thessalonians 5:16–18 (WEB)

“Always rejoice. Pray without ceasing. In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus toward you.”

As you spend time with family, gather for worship, or find a quiet moment this weekend, please continue to stand in the gap. Let us remain faithful in prayer, trusting the Lord with the people and circumstances we cannot personally reach.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
