TO THE THRONE · TTTPC-0001-03 · THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 12, 2026

Persecuted Church Prayer Letter

Plateau State, Nigeria · Standing with grieving families and Christian farming communities

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

This week, please remember families in Nigeria’s Plateau State who are grieving after another night of violence. These are not merely distant reports. Behind them are loved ones mourning, neighbors afraid to return to ordinary life, and believers seeking safety to worship and work.

Families Grieving After Attacks in Plateau State

Bel village area, Barkin Ladi; Lakas/Panyam area, Plateau State, Nigeria

Local reporting dated November 9th says gunmen attacked the Bel village area of Barkin Ladi on November 8th, killing three civilians and one soldier and injuring others. International Christian Concern (ICC), reporting November 10th, also describes a separate attack in the Lakas/Panyam area, bringing its reported total for the two incidents to six. Other reporting gives a lower combined toll of five. These figures are disputed and must not be added together.

Residents told ICC that Christian farming communities are frightened to return to their fields or gather for worship. Their fear and need for protection deserve our attention. At the same time, the religious identities of every victim, the assailants’ identities, and the motives for these particular attacks have not been independently established. Allegations about attackers and land seizure remain attributed claims, not proven findings. Local accounts concerning the Bel attack draw in part on shared community testimony rather than wholly independent confirmation.

Prayer Focus

For grieving families and the wounded: Please lift up those who have buried loved ones, including the family of a young victim. Let’s pray for the injured to receive treatment and recover, and for the Lord’s comfort to surround those carrying grief and fear.

For safety and practical care: Remember children, families, and Christian farming communities who fear further violence. Pray for safe access to homes, fields, churches, medical care, and daily necessities.

For truth, justice, and peace: Ask the Lord to give wisdom and integrity to local authorities, protection to vulnerable communities, and reconciliation among neighbors. Pray for truthful investigations and accountability without retaliation against innocent people.

For those responsible: Let’s pray that those who committed violence would turn from it, repent, and come to saving faith in Jesus Christ. May the Lord bring an end to further harm and transform hearts.

Hebrews 13:3 (World English Bible, Updated Edition) — Remember those who are in bonds, as bound with them, and those who are ill-treated, since you are also in the body.

As we remember these families in Plateau State, may we also continue lifting up believers and vulnerable communities elsewhere whose suffering receives little attention. Let us not forget those who are hurting simply because the world has turned its attention somewhere else.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
