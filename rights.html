Rights & Use

Restored Daily Ministries™ exists to point people to Jesus Christ. We want these resources to be shared for encouragement and ministry, while also protecting the original materials from misuse.

Ministry Name & Branding

Restored Daily Ministries™, including the ministry name, visual identity, ministry logo, original graphics, and related branding, is used and maintained by Restored Daily Ministries.

Original Content

Unless otherwise noted, original written devotionals, journal entries, website wording, podcast materials, video messages, graphics, and other original ministry resources created for Restored Daily Ministries are protected by copyright from the moment they are created and fixed in a tangible form.

Iron Sharpener™ & Scripture Use Notice

Iron Sharpener™ and related Scripture study tools were prayerfully created and developed by Restored Daily Ministries™ to support biblical encouragement, discipleship, Scripture study, teaching, and ministry use centered on Jesus Christ.

Unless otherwise noted, Scripture quotations used within Iron Sharpener™ and related ministry tools are from the World English Bible (WEB), which is placed in the public domain and may be freely used, shared, copied, and distributed.

Restored Daily Ministries™ encourages believers, teachers, ministries, and individuals to engage with Scripture deeply and freely. You are welcome to use the WEB translation as much or as little as you desire for personal study, teaching, ministry, and encouragement.

While Scripture itself is freely shared for the glory of God, the original ministry materials, layouts, branding, visual presentation, study environments, graphics, custom tool structures, written ministry content, and overall ministry platform developed by Restored Daily Ministries™ remain protected intellectual property and ministry work product.

Sharing Our Content

You are welcome to share links to our website, devotional archive, podcast episodes, YouTube videos, prayer form, Iron Sharpener™, and ministry resources for encouragement, outreach, and ministry purposes.

You May

  • Share links to the ministry website and tools.
  • Freely read and study Scripture.
  • Quote brief excerpts with attribution.
  • Use the WEB translation for personal or ministry purposes.

Please Do Not

  • Copy, repost, sell, or redistribute full Restored Daily Ministries materials as your own.
  • Repackage or redistribute the ministry’s original tools, layouts, branding, or written content as your own.
  • Mirror, clone, or commercially reproduce the ministry platform in full.
  • Modify, rebrand, or remove Restored Daily Ministries™ identification from original resources.
  • Use the ministry name, logo, or materials in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, or ownership without permission.
  • Use written, audio, video, or visual materials for commercial purposes without written permission.

Third-Party Materials

Scripture quotations, embedded YouTube content, podcast platform features, third-party platform logos, and any other third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners and are used according to their applicable terms, permissions, licenses, or public-domain status.

Permission Requests

For permission to use Restored Daily Ministries materials beyond sharing a link, please contact us through the ministry website or official ministry channels.

All Scripture, encouragement, and teaching shared through this ministry are intended to glorify Jesus Christ and encourage those seeking Him.
