Why Support?

Restored Daily Ministries exists to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ through daily devotionals, Bible teaching, prayer ministry, discipleship resources, preaching, and digital outreach.

Our mission is to reach people who feel forgotten, discouraged, overlooked, or convinced that God can no longer use them. Through Christ Jesus, we believe every life still has purpose.

Your support helps make this ministry possible and allows biblical encouragement, prayer, teaching, and discipleship resources to continue reaching people who need the hope of Christ.

What Your Support Helps Provide

Every gift helps further the mission of pointing people toward Christ Jesus through biblical encouragement, discipleship, prayer, and teaching.
