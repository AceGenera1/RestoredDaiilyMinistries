You Are Welcome Here

Restored Daily Ministries exists for people who have felt forgotten, disqualified, written off, weary, or too broken to be used by God. In Christ Jesus, your story is not over.

This community is not about perfection. It is about walking together in prayer, encouragement, Scripture, and the restoring grace of Jesus Christ.
