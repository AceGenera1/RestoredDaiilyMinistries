TO THE THRONE · TTT-0005 · November 23, 2026

Weekly Intercession Letter

Standing in the gap for those in need. Turning awareness into intercession.

To the Body of Christ,

Brothers and sisters in Christ,

As we enter another week, I want to invite you to remember five communities facing loss, displacement, danger, or urgent needs for shelter. From flood-affected families to those living amid conflict, each situation represents people who deserve our compassion and prayers. May we remember them faithfully before the Lord.

01 Broward County, Florida — Flooding

Broward County, Florida, United States

Heavy rainfall and flooding on November 18th disrupted homes, roads, and schools in Broward County, Florida. Broward County Public Schools announced districtwide closures for November 18th and 19th because of flooding and continuing severe weather. Local reporting described damaged homes and vehicles and emergency declarations; families now face the work of recovering and restoring safe daily routines.

Prayer Focus: Let’s remember families whose homes or vehicles were damaged, students and teachers whose schools were closed, emergency workers, and neighbors who need practical help.

Isaiah 43:2 (WEB Updated / WEBU) — When you pass through the waters, I will be with you, and through the rivers, they will not overflow you. When you walk through the fire, you will not be burned, and flame will not scorch you.

02 Tana River County, Kenya — Flooding and Displacement

Tana River County and other affected counties, Kenya

Flooding across parts of Kenya has damaged homes, farmland, roads, and water facilities, with especially serious disruption reported in Tana River County. Kenyan reporting on November 21st, citing a Kenya Red Cross situation assessment, described six deaths across affected counties and 1,141 households affected. Those figures are attributed to the reported assessment rather than independently verified totals, while families face urgent needs for shelter, clean water, and recovery support.

Prayer Focus: Please lift up bereaved and displaced families, communities needing safe water and sanitation, farmers whose livelihoods have been affected, and relief workers trying to reach isolated households.

Deuteronomy 15:11 (WEB Updated / WEBU) — For the poor will never cease out of the land. Therefore I command you to surely open your hand to your brother, to your needy, and to your poor, in your land.

03 Bangladesh — Sattola Slum Fire

Sattola, Dhaka, Bangladesh

On November 18th, a fire swept through homes in the Sattola slum in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Fire Service officials reported that eight firefighting units brought the blaze under control at approximately 11:15 a.m., while subsequent local reporting described more than 100 families losing shelter and belongings. Initial officials reported no deaths or injuries, and the cause had not yet been determined; affected families need immediate necessities and help rebuilding.

Prayer Focus: Let’s pray for families who lost homes and possessions, children and older residents needing protection, and those providing food, clothing, safe shelter, and longer-term rebuilding assistance.

Matthew 25:35–36 (WEB Updated / WEBU) — for I was hungry and you gave me food to eat. I was thirsty and you gave me drink. I was a stranger and you took me in. I was naked and you clothed me. I was sick and you visited me. I was in prison and you came to me.

04 Khartoum Area, Sudan — Drone Strikes

Khartoum, Omdurman and Jebel Aulia, Sudan

Drone strikes reported on November 22nd placed civilians in the Khartoum area of Sudan in danger. Sudan Tribune reported on November 23rd that local accounts described at least four civilians killed, including a child; the United Nations separately confirmed damage to a UN building while staff were inside. The UN statement does not independently establish the reported death toll, and responsibility for the strikes remains disputed.

Prayer Focus: Please remember grieving families, injured civilians, children exposed to violence, humanitarian staff and facilities, and everyone seeking safety and peace amid the conflict.

Romans 12:18 (WEB Updated / WEBU) — If it is possible, as much as it is up to you, be at peace with all men.

05 Marin County, California — Storm Shelter Preparations

Marin County and other coastal California counties, United States

As storms and unusually high king tides were forecast for Northern California, Marin County announced on November 20th that emergency overnight shelter would be available November 24th through 26th for unhoused people and families. On November 22nd, California emergency officials also described advance positioning of rescue resources in Marin, San Francisco, Alameda, and Humboldt counties. These are preparations for forecast hazards, not evidence that the anticipated storm damage has already occurred.

Prayer Focus: Let’s lift up people sleeping outdoors, families seeking shelter, outreach teams and emergency responders, and communities preparing for possible flooding, high tides, and power disruptions.

Galatians 6:2 (WEB Updated / WEBU) — Bear one another’s burdens, and so fulfill the law of Christ.

These are only five of the many people and communities in need of prayer. There are countless others whose struggles may never reach our news feeds, but they are not forgotten by the Lord. May we remain faithful in intercession, remembering both the needs represented here and those known only to our Heavenly Father.

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

In Christ,
Brother Kris
