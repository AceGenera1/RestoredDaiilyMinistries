TTT-0001

October 26, 2026 • Turning awareness into intercession.

Purpose & Source Standard

This record provides the public sources supporting the factual context used in this week's To The Throne intercession letter and video. Each source is paired with a short explanation of what it establishes. Primary and credible local sources are prioritized where available, consequential claims are independently corroborated when reasonably possible, and copies of the same underlying report are not treated as independent confirmation.

01 — Drone Attacks Kill and Injure Civilians

Amiet Market / Abyei Region • Sudan–South Sudan Border

ACTIVE

UNISFA confirms drone attacks near Amiet Market and Todach on October 19 killed and injured civilians, including women and children; casualty verification remains ongoing and people remain missing. The UN Security Council condemned the attacks and called for investigation and accountability.

03 — Humanitarian Conditions Remain Critical

Gaza Strip

CONTINUING INTERCESSION

Shortages also threatened health and water services.

ACTIVE

WHO reported nine linked Kenyan cases with no transmission outside that cluster at its cutoff, while Nigeria's imported Abuja case remained under investigation; Kenya later announced a second death among its nine cases.

Thursday, October 29 — Persecuted Church Prayer Letter: Ethiopia

Letter TTTPC-0001-01 · Merti and Shirka districts, Arsi Zone, Oromia Region. The Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church reported that attacks on October 20 and 24 killed 60 people, left more than 20 seriously wounded, and burned 20 homes. Those figures are church-reported; casualty estimates vary across reporting. Eyewitness accounts and conflict-event analysis document serious violence, but the perpetrators and motives are not independently established. Do not treat repeated retellings of the Church's statement as independent confirmations of its casualty count.

Associated Press — eyewitness reporting

Associated Press, October 27: Interviews a resident who helped transport injured people and a priest who saw wounded people at a hospital. Adds witness context, not an independent 60-death census.

African Press Agency — Holy Synod statement

APA News, October 26: Reports the Church's account of 14 deaths in Merti on October 20 and 46 in Shirka on October 24. These are attributed Church figures.

ACLED — conflict-event analysis

ACLED, Arsi Zone analysis: Provides a conflict-event dataset and regional historical context. Its incident-level estimates are not directly interchangeable with the Church's combined figure, and actor identification remains uncertain.

The Abay Times — local Ethiopian reporting

The Abay Times, October 26: Covers the Synod statement and differing local witness reports. Note the variation rather than silently merging the reported tolls.

World Council of Churches — church response

WCC statement, October 27: Calls for protection, care, accountability and reconciliation. It is a church response, not a separate forensic verification of casualties.

Prayer focus: Comfort for the grieving, healing for the wounded, refuge and provision for displaced families, protection and strength for local believers, fair investigation and peace, and repentance and salvation for those responsible.
