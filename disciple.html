Lessons from a Disciple

Reflections written to encourage believers, strengthen faith, and point hearts toward the Lord Jesus Christ.

Lessons from a Disciple is a daily journal of walking with Jesus Christ.

These writings are not from someone who has everything figured out, but from a disciple learning day by day through Scripture, prayer, conviction, failure, grace, and the leading of the Holy Spirit.

My prayer is that these reflections encourage believers, strengthen faith, and point hearts toward the Lord Jesus Christ.
