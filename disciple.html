Lessons from a Disciple

These are raw reflections from my walk with Jesus Christ — lessons learned through Scripture, prayer, conviction, weakness, grace, and the daily work of the Holy Spirit.

May 11, 2026 • 12:21 AM

I have made this note because I want to start maintaining a daily Bible journal entry. I don’t know where this is gonna go or how it’s gonna go. But I know a lot of people say that it’s important to journal and I’ve had a stirring in my heart to journal. There’s plenty of physical Bibles that allow you to journal next to the scripture, but my handwriting is terrible, and there’s not very many apps that accommodate exactly what I’m looking for as far as note taking alongside the word. So I just settled with making a note, and I prayed to the Lord that I wanted to hear him more clearly, and I wanted him to reveal his word to me and teach me. Immediately following that short heartfelt prayer. I heard the Holy Spirit speak to me to turn to Acts chapter 11:17 which states. “And since God gave these Gentiles the same gift he gave us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I to stand in God’s way?”” the Lord began to speak to me and reminded me of a conversation that I had with my mother. Her boyfriend Walter wants to marry her. He’s honestly a great man, and God is doing a work in his life. We originally agreed that he would stay sober for a year before he could marry our mother, but the Lord God has spoken to his heart and has advised him to not live in sin. I don’t want him living in sin either and to be honest with you, this is an answer to prayer. Because him and my mother live together, and they’re not married which any believer should know that is a sin. Because a man and woman living together are going to have sex. Which I know is outside of the will of God for anyone in Christ. But I never tried to preach that in a hateful or judgmental way. Through a lot of prayer and discussion with the Lord he has reminded me time and time again that I don’t need to correct everybody, and he’s the one that cleans them up and it’s his Holy Spirit that does the work in the life of any individual. I can’t save anyone, and I can’t change anyone. I must remember this as I continue forward in life. I don’t wanna get in the way of God in any individual’s life. That’s what I know. I gave them my blessing, not that it matters. But I guess it does matter to me, and maybe it matters to God too. There’s an old gospel song that says it matters to him about you. God cares for me greatly, and I must remember that as well, and remember that I don’t have to be perfect and not to put more pressure on myself than God would ever put on me. That’s all for now.

May 11, 2026 • 1:08 PM

The Lord had me read Romans chapter 1 through 6 this afternoon before I get into anything else. As I've just been resting this morning.

As I was reading through Romans chapters 1 through 6, there are a few things that stood out to me. The first one being that in the first chapter of Romans, Paul mentions how he was sent to preach the gospel, to be a witness for the gospel, three times. I really feel like that spoke to my heart. It was like a pricking of my heart, as in that's what the Lord God is wanting me to do with the ministry that he gave me of restored daily ministries. I don't fully understand all that he wants me to do with that ministry, or how he wants me to go about it, 100% clear as of yet. What I do know is that he has the plan, and I just need to walk in it, and it will become more and more clear. I really feel he wants me to go live and start preaching. I do think that that's going to be a big part of the ministry, and that's going to be how he uses me to reach people. I need to look into how to do that on multiple platforms at one time.

One of the other things that stood out to me in my reading is in Romans chapter 5, verse 17, which states that if we receive God's free gift of righteousness, we will triumph over sin and death through Christ Jesus. I think that this stands out to me so much because I myself, as a believer in Christ, have struggled with sin. I'm realizing more and more, thank God, through the message of the cross. That Jesus died not only to give us eternal life but to give us abundant life. In John chapter 10, verse 10, Jesus says, "I came to give you life and life abundantly." Well if he's going to lay his life down for us as he did to give us abundant, victorious, and eternal life, then I don't think I should continue to struggle with sin as in being a slave to it every single day. That doesn't make sense to me. I don't believe I fully understand this but I do believe, again, that through the preaching of the message of the cross and the teachings of that, predominantly through Jimmy Swigert Ministries, I am understanding more and more how to walk in victory through the power of the Holy Spirit, not my own strength or power or understanding but that exclusively of the Holy Spirit.

one final note. In Romans chapter 6, verse 16, it states that you become a slave to whatever you choose to obey. In other words, the Holy Spirit is saying that if we choose to obey our sinful desires, our sinful lusts, or the sin of this world, the flesh and the devil, we are making ourselves a slave to that. If we choose every single day, as the Lord Jesus Christ says in the Gospels, to deny ourselves and pick up our cross, every single day, we are choosing to align and assign ourselves to Christ Jesus. Therefore, we are able to live out the victorious life and walk in the righteousness of Christ. That's what I believe the Holy Spirit is trying to say to believers through the Apostle Paul in these first six chapters of Romans. Never have I understood how powerful the Book of Romans is. I don't think in my entire life, I've had revelation and breakthrough before in reading Romans at different points over the last couple of years, but it is ringing so loudly and so clearly now. For that I am thankful to my Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. I am thankful to the Heavenly Father of my Lord, the Lord Jesus Christ, and I am so thankful for and to the Holy Spirit. Thank you, Lord Jesus. Thank you. Amen

May 12, 2026 • 11:45 PM

I continued in my reading of Romans, and I read Romans chapter seven. As I felt prompted by the Holy Spirit, I actually started reading it earlier this morning, but felt led to stop and go visit my friend Eric and his wife, and that was a good time. Now this evening, after I finished writing my devotional, the Holy Spirit spoke to me and said to read Romans chapter seven again. It's a powerful chapter, and there's so much that we could unpack. This evening, the focus was predominantly reading through the whole chapter. My attention and my mind was brought to Romans chapter seven, verse six, which talks about a few key points:

1. We have been released from the law because we died to it and are no longer captive to its power.
2. Because of that, because of us being dead to the law and its power, we can now serve God not by obeying the law but in the new way of living in the spirit.

What does it really look like to live in the spirit? A few things that I've learned in my time of walking with the Lord, especially in the last two years, are that the Holy Spirit dwells inside of us. We, as believers, know that the Holy Spirit dwells inside of us, but most of us, and I myself for a long time, didn't understand this particular part. Yes, he dwells inside of us by sealing himself inside of us when we come to Christ Jesus and receive new life. We are born again in Christ Jesus by receiving him as Savior. We are born again. Through that process, the Holy Spirit comes in, and scripture says, "He seals himself inside of us and joins himself with our spirit." Thus we become one with the living God through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Here's the thing that we miss. It's something that I missed for so long, it's so important, and it's something that the Holy Spirit wants us all to know: if we surrender ourselves to the Holy Spirit, then we will become His vessels and His instruments, and He will literally fill us with His power on a daily basis and live through us. I was just reminded of that, and I felt the Holy Spirit wanted me to jot this down to teach others that He desires to live through believers in Christ. It should be as though Christians are literally vessels and instruments for Christ Jesus to live through on the earth. Everyone who encounters a Christian should encounter Christ through them. It doesn't mean you have to be perfect. It just means you have to be surrendered and obedient, yielded and following. Is another way to put it. If you do that, you will change the world for Christ. That's what I'm realizing, and that's what I've been learning. That's what I've been living and that's what I've been experiencing for almost two years now, the better part of two years, I should say, predominantly over the last two years. I felt led of the Holy Spirit to jot that down today, and I really hope that this journal entry encourages someone truly. May God bless us all Amen.

May 16, 2026 • 1:59 AM

The following are portions of writings from what was supposed to be a devotional on Romans chapter 7 verse 23, but it ended up being way too long, and the dictation that I use actually cut off, but when I went to delete it, I felt the Holy Spirit say to save that for this journal entry here. Like I said, it's pretty extensive and it would have been too long for a devotional. I felt like the Holy Spirit wanted me to rewrite the devotional, but again, not to get rid of this writing here and put it in this journal entry. That's what I've done.

I just wanted to note that these journal entries are meant to be raw and real because that's what they are. They're my real lived-out experiences with the Lord Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit, both of whom I try my best to follow and be guided by in this life. I hope it encourages someone, and I hope you all enjoy it. May God bless you dearly. In Jesus' name.

The believer in Christ Jesus must know that they can walk in victory every single day of their life. Victory over sin, victory over this world, victory over the flesh, and victory over the devil is possible only through faith in Christ Jesus.

I know that this world likes to tell you that you are a sinner saved by grace and that you all have sinned and fallen short of the glory of God. While both of these statements are true, they have been absolutely taken out of context to justify people living in sin and condoning acts of sin and lifestyles of sin every single day of their life. That is not what the scripture is saying. That is not what Jesus died for, and that is not what Jesus would condone and stand for, and neither should you as a believer in Christ Jesus.

You must be certain that one day, and one day soon, Jesus is coming back to judge all of us. The earth will be judged, and those that walk in wickedness will be judged, and those of us that are in Christ Jesus are going to be judged as well for what we did with our salvation. Now, do you honestly think that if you've lived a life of sin and constantly condoned sin in your life that Jesus is going to be okay with that? Absolutely not. Absolutely not.

Jesus desires for you to walk in victory and freedom because he died to give you that. He died a brutal death on the cross, to where he was beaten so brutally bad that he was absolutely unrecognizable. Some scholars even believe that his head swelled so bad and his beard was ripped from his face. It was absolutely brutal! Do you really think that Jesus died so that you could just continue living in your old ways? No! Remember, the Bible says something about a dog returning to his vomit. You don't want to be that guy or that woman doing that. You don't want to be the one that keeps struggling and straddling the fence of sin, addiction, fornication, or whatever horrible, reckless lifestyle Jesus saved you out of. You don't need to live like that anymore, beloved! Jesus died to give you the victory. I can't stress that enough.

Not only that, he gave you the Holy Spirit of God, which is the same power that raised Jesus from the dead. If he can raise Jesus from the dead, he can help you to walk in victory. We've spoken about this a lot, but it's so true and it's worth repeating over and over because most people can't seem to understand it or comprehend it. The Holy Spirit of God dwells inside of you. The power that raised Jesus from the dead, again, that's worth mentioning. If he raised Jesus from the dead, how can he not help you to overcome addiction? Overcome lust? Overcome drinking? Overcome gambling? Overcome overeating? Overcome being angry? Overcome being depressed? Overcome being anxious? Overcome being worried or whatever else you can think of, fill in the blank. How can he not do that? He raised a man from the dead.

Here's the thing: we don't truly rely on the power of the Holy Spirit. Some because we've never been taught, and some because we just don't understand. I'm not here to bash anybody, but I'm here to get you to understand that if you are in Christ Jesus, you have the power of the living God inside of you. Therefore, Christ Jesus can literally live through you through the power of the Holy Spirit. If you rely on the Holy Spirit, He will literally fill you with His power to walk in victory and to never go back to the sin that you have struggled with time and time again. You don't have to return to that prison cell anymore, beloved.

May 16, 2026 • 9:13 AM

The Lord had me read Luke chapter 14 three times this morning. The first time, reading it all the way through. The second time, highlighting key verses. The first verse highlighted was verse 11, "For all those who exalt themselves will be humbled, and those who humble themselves will be exalted." The second verse highlighted is verse 17, "At the time of the banquet, he sent his servant to tell those who had been invited, 'Come, for everything is now ready.'" The third and final verse highlighted is verse 22, "Sir, the servant said, 'What you ordered has been done, but there is still room.'"

The Lord spoke these words to my heart. There is plenty of room at my banquet table, and all of those who will humble themselves before me will be invited into my banquet and feast with me for eternity. We must remember to remain humble before the Lord God each and every day. We must keep ourselves humble through the power of the Holy Spirit. It's not through our own strength or our own understanding, but it's exclusively through the power of the Holy Spirit, because we can't do it on our own.

The Lord God desires for all of us to be at his banquet table, to feast with him, to be with him and the Father in heaven for all of eternity, never to be separated from them again. The Lord is coming back one day, and one day very soon. I urge all of you to humble yourselves before the Lord. I myself am urging myself, through the power of the Holy Spirit, to remain humble before the Lord so that I can continue to be used of him and be with him at his banquet table. May the Lord be lifted up in all of our lives, and may the Lord be glorified in each and every one of our lives this very day. Amen.
