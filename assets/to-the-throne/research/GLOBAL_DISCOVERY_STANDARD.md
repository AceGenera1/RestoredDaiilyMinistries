# To The Throne — Global Weekly Discovery Standard
Approved editorial direction: November 9, 2026.

## Mission
Find the most substantial, timely, responsibly verifiable human crises worldwide for compassionate Christian intercession. This is not a breaking-news ranking or a quota by country.

## Discovery pass
Every Sunday research window, deliberately scan the United States (including rural/underreported communities), Canada, Latin America/Caribbean, Europe, North Africa, sub-Saharan Africa, Middle East, Central/South/East/Southeast Asia, Oceania and Pacific islands. Include overlooked regions and people groups. Search primary humanitarian agencies, local authorities, trusted regional outlets, faith/persecution sources when appropriate, and disaster/health response agencies. Do not use news volume as a proxy for human need.

## Candidate scoring (editorial judgment, not fabricated metrics)
Assess severity, people at immediate risk, urgency of prayer needs, recency or meaningful new escalation, verifiability, and underreported suffering. Prefer five genuinely distinct new weekly matters. Seek geographic and category breadth; do not enforce artificial continent quotas. No country is banned: Gaza, Sudan, Ukraine or another continuing crisis may return for a genuinely new substantial development, not merely because they dominate headlines.

## Anti-repetition check
Before selection, compare candidates with the previous four weekly editions and the continuing-intercession ledger. Flag same country + same underlying situation + no material new event as continuing, not new. Avoid repeating the same disaster/conflict category when other comparably urgent verified crises exist. Preserve significant continuing needs for Wednesday review and internal tracking.

## Evidence and publication
Prioritize original official/local/humanitarian reporting, with independent corroboration where feasible; attribute figures and unresolved claims. Never invent sources, event dates, maps, or independent verification. Unique complete WEB or labeled WEBU passage per selected matter. Keep approval, visual signoff and external-publication gates separate. Wednesday only on user request this week; do not send or publish a graphic merely because a template is prepared.
