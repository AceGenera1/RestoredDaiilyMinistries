To The Throne • Weekly Recording Notes

TTT-0001

Week of October 26, 2026 • Five verified matters with prayer focus and Scripture for intercession.

How These Notes Are Used

These are the weekly notes used to guide the To The Throne intercession session. Each matter includes the verified details, unresolved questions, specific prayer focus, and a Scripture passage to help lead the Body of Christ in prayer. There is no required runtime for these sessions; the report serves the prayer, and the prayer is given the time it needs.

01 — Drone Attacks Kill and Injure Civilians

Amiet Market / Abyei Region • Sudan–South Sudan Border

ACTIVE

Key Verified Details

  • October 19 drone attacks killed and injured civilians.
  • Casualty verification continues; people remain missing.
  • The UN Security Council called for investigation and accountability.

Watch / Uncertainty

Final casualty count and responsibility remain unresolved.

Prayer Focus

  • Grieving families and the wounded
  • Those still missing and families awaiting answers
  • Protection of civilians and restraint against retaliation
  • Truthful investigation, accountability, peace and stability
Psalm 34:18 (WEB) — “The Lord is near to those who have a broken heart, and saves those who have a crushed spirit.”

02 — Military Reports 54 Civilian Bodies Found

Tagadayat / Ménaka Region • Mali

ACTIVE

Key Verified Details

  • Mali's military reported 54 civilian bodies found.
  • Nine survivors were evacuated to Ménaka.
  • ISGS attribution remains qualified.

Watch / Uncertainty

Full casualty and displacement totals and independent verification remain incomplete.

Prayer Focus

  • Bereaved families and healing for the nine survivors
  • Protection for exposed communities
  • Aid and safety for displaced families
  • Wisdom and restraint for authorities; an end to further killing
Psalm 9:9 (WEB) — “The Lord will also be a high tower for the oppressed; a high tower in times of trouble.”

03 — Humanitarian Conditions Remain Critical

Gaza Strip

CONTINUING INTERCESSION

Key Verified Details

  • About 950,000 displaced people face medium-to-high flood risk.
  • Shelter supplies could partly assist only 18% of families in need.

Watch / Uncertainty

Access and winterization capacity remain developing.

Prayer Focus

  • Protection of civilians, especially children
  • Food, clean water, shelter and medicine
  • Humanitarian access and safety for aid workers
  • Healing, restraint and peace
Psalm 145:14 (WEB) — “The Lord upholds all who fall, and raises up all those who are bowed down.”

04 — Water, Health and Civilian Needs Intensify

Taiz • Yemen

ACTIVE

Key Verified Details

  • Water systems serving about three million people are threatened.
  • About 120,000 people are displaced; 98 health facilities are impaired.

Watch / Uncertainty

The reported 64 killed and 173 wounded remains an attributed monitoring-office report.

Prayer Focus

  • Grieving and wounded families; protection of children
  • Water, fuel, medicine and oxygen
  • Hospitals, health workers and safe humanitarian access
  • Protection of civilians and an end to violence
Psalm 46:1 (WEB) — “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”

05 — Multi-Country Bundibugyo Ebola Outbreak

Kenya / DRC / Nigeria

ACTIVE

Key Verified Details

  • WHO: DRC 9,704 cases and 4,687 deaths as of October 20.
  • Kenya: nine linked cases, later a second death; Nigeria: one imported case.

Watch / Uncertainty

Nigeria's exposure source and the regional trajectory remain unknown.

Prayer Focus

  • Patients, grieving families and affected communities
  • Healthcare, burial, laboratory and contact-tracing teams
  • Containment and protection of vulnerable communities
  • Wisdom for public-health leaders; integrity and success in vaccine research
Psalm 41:3 (WEB) — “The Lord will sustain him on his sickbed, and restore him from his bed of illness.”
