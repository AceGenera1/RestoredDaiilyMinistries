Faith Friday: Prayer Never Ceases

Do not let these needs fade from memory. Keep standing in the gap.

As we enter the weekend, let us continue to bring before the Lord all the matters we have lifted up this week. Their needs do not disappear when the week comes to an end.

Pray for the persecuted, the suffering, the displaced, the grieving, the sick, and the lost. Ask the Lord for His protection, comfort, healing, provision, justice, and salvation. Let us remain faithful in intercession, trusting that the Lord is at work even when we cannot see it.

1 Corinthians 15:58 (WEB)

“Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the Lord’s work, because you know that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.”

The news cycle may move on, but prayer never ceases.

Continue praying through the week’s matters using the Weekly Intercession Archive. This Faith Friday invitation is not an additional news report.
